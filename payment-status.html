Waiting for your MoMo approval

When live checkout is connected, Paystack will send a payment request to your mobile phone. Approve it with your Mobile Money PIN, then return here to check the verified order status.

What to do while a payment is pending

  1. Check your phone for the Mobile Money approval prompt.
  2. Approve the request with your MoMo PIN if the amount and merchant details are correct.
  3. Keep this page open while the payment provider confirms the result. Avoid paying again while it is still pending.
