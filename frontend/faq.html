Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about shopping with Gid’s. We’ll update this page as GBHPL confirms product, delivery, and payment details.

Products

What products can I order?

The current shop preview lists Gid’s Organic Apple Cider Vinegar in 500 ml and 1000 ml sizes. GBHPL still needs to confirm the final launch catalogue, availability, and product information.

See product details.

Are the prices shown final?

The listed prices, GH₵105 for 500 ml and GH₵170 for 1000 ml, are provisional and need GBHPL approval before launch.

Where can I find ingredients and usage information?

Only details approved by GBHPL should appear in the final product information. Ask the team if you need information that is not yet listed. This store does not make medical claims.

Delivery

Do you deliver outside Cape Coast?

Delivery is intended to be available across Ghana. GBHPL is still confirming fees, timing, and fulfillment details for destinations. The final checkout must show the delivery charge and full total before payment.

How much does delivery cost, and when will it arrive?

The charge and delivery estimate have not been confirmed yet. We’ll show the confirmed details before you authorize payment; we won’t guess a delivery fee.

Ordering & payment

Can I pay with Mobile Money?

Paystack Mobile Money is the planned payment method. It is not connected in this preview, so please do not enter real checkout details here. Live MoMo payments will be available only after GBHPL completes Paystack setup.

When is an order confirmed?

In the live shop, an order should be shown as paid only after the payment is verified by the store’s backend. A return to the website alone does not prove payment succeeded.

What if my payment is pending or fails?

Do not retry while a payment is still pending. Contact GBHPL with your order reference for help. Never share your Mobile Money PIN or one-time authorization code.

Contact customer support.

Returns & help

What is your returns or refunds policy?

GBHPL’s return, exchange, and refund policy is awaiting approval. It must be published before live sales begin. Please contact the team if you need help with an order.

How can I contact GBHPL?

Email info@gbhpl.net with your question. For an order issue, include its reference and never send payment PINs or passwords.
